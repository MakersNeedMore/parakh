Project Overview Dataset: FaceForensics++

Deepfake Face Detection System

A specialized platform engineered to detect deep fake pictures, morphed images, and synthetic or AI-manipulated video frames. The system applies a binary classification model to distinguish between authentic and manipulated facial media. For video streams, it sequentially inspects frames, aggregates confidence scores, and highlights segments that appear suspicious.

Binary Classification

Distinguishes authentic from manipulated faces with confidence scoring.

Temporal Frame Aggregation

Sequentially parses MP4 frames to isolate high-risk video segments.

Attention Heatmaps

Visualises spatial facial regions influencing model predictions.

Robustness Evaluation

Resilience testing across JPEG compression and Gaussian blur.
